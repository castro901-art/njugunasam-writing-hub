Why We Called It Tiff

When we started developing our intelligent systems at Trendify, we wanted a name that was simple, memorable and distinctive. Something that could represent more than just another AI chatbot. We wanted a name that could grow alongside the technology itself.

That's how Tiff came about.

Of course, the name has attracted its fair share of curiosity. Some people have jokingly suggested that I named the system after a girl called Tiffany I was dating. Others have been convinced there must be some interesting personal story behind it.

I won't lie; the rumours are entertaining.

But the actual explanation is much more connected to what we are building.

TIFF stands for Trendify Intelligent Futuristic Framework.

Each part of the name reflects an idea behind our original vision:

  • Trendify represents the company and the people behind the technology.
  • Intelligent reflects our focus on developing systems that can understand, reason, assist and perform useful tasks.
  • Futuristic represents our ambition to build technology that anticipates how people and businesses will interact with intelligent systems.
  • Framework reflects the underlying foundation through which we develop and evolve our AI capabilities.

The name was never intended to be limited to a single chatbot. It represented a broader technological vision: creating an intelligent framework that could support different systems, applications and experiences.

Over time, that vision has continued to evolve. What began as an idea has grown into a wider ecosystem of AI products and technologies under Trendify Labs. Tiff has become the identity through which many people experience our work, while the underlying technology continues to develop.

And perhaps that is why the name has lasted. It is short enough to remember, distinctive enough to recognise and broad enough to grow with us.

So, to settle the question once and for all: No, I wasn't building an AI system for Tiffany. I was building Tiff.

Although, judging by how often I have had to explain this, perhaps we should have considered the marketing implications before choosing the name.

Tiff: Trendify Intelligent Futuristic Framework.
